    Pick a color and build a palette.

    Create, compare, and save color palettes locally.

    How to use

    1. Choose a color with the picker, enter HEX, RGB, or HSL values, or sample from the screen or an image.
    2. Choose a harmony to generate matching colors. Open Contrast checker to compare two swatches.
    3. Copy a color or the whole palette, export a PNG, or name and save the palette to reopen later.

    Use Back up JSON to save a copy of your library. Restore JSON adds missing palettes and keeps your existing ones.
